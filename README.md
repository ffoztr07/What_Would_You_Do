# **WhatWouldYouDo.info \- AI-Powered English Learning**

Welcome to WhatWouldYouDo.info, an AI-supported project designed to help you improve your practical English skills through interactive, real-life scenarios.

## **🚀 Our Mission**

Our goal is to make learning English more fun, practical, interactive, and efficient for everyone.

## **How It Works**

This tool is simple to use but powerful in its approach:

1. **Get a Scenario:** The AI presents you with a random scenario (e.g., "You are offered a job with a higher salary but less job satisfaction. What do you choose?").  
2. **Give Your Response:** You type your response in English, just as you would in a real conversation.  
3. **Receive AI Feedback:** Our AI analyzes your response and provides two key things:  
   * **A Score:** You'll get a rating from 1 to 5 on the quality and appropriateness of your answer.  
   * **Corrections:** The AI corrects any errors in your sentences and provides examples of correct phrasing, helping you learn from your mistakes immediately.

## **🌱 Help Us Improve**

This project is continuously evolving. By providing responses to scenarios, you are not only practicing your English but also directly contributing to the development and improvement of our future AI model. Both your feedback and the data we collect are essential for taking this project to the next level.
